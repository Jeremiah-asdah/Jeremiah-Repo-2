Why Being Healthy Matters

What is healthy living for children? It means helping them to make positive choices in their daily lives, choices that help them feel their best every day. Having a nutritious diet, being physically active, having sufficient rest, and keeping our mental and emotional wellbeing in check all work in tandem with each other to keep us all healthy in mind and body. Simple everyday habits can shape our tomorrow, giving us energy to learn, play, grow, and enjoy every moment.

This site has been made to support everyone, students, parents and whānau alike using practical information, fun activities and helpful resources to encourage healthy lifestyles. We hope there will be something to inspire you, whether it be ways to stay active, activities to enjoy together as a family, or tips to get a good night’s rest, to name a few.

Something to keep in mind is that healthy living requires you to be perfect every day, it just means making small, positive choices that help in the long run. Every healthy habit built continues to create a stronger, happier future. We hope this site can motivate you to try new things with the little ones, stay active, eat well, and enjoy the slow and steady road to lifelong healthy and wellbeing.

Every healthy choice you make today helps you grow stronger tomorrow. Small steps every day can lead to big achievements. Believe in yourself because you are capable of amazing things! Your body is your superpower, so treat it with care and kindness. Be active, eat well, smile often, and enjoy the journey to a healthier you.
