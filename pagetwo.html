Good kai, happy kids! Simple ways to help tamariki eat well and grow strong.

Why does healthy eating matter for children?

The food tamariki eat gives them the energy and nutrients they need to learn, play, grow, and stay healthy. Eating a variety of nutritious kai, including fruits, vegetables, wholegrains, protein, and dairy or alternatives, helps support their growing bodies and keeps them energised throughout the day. Healthy eating can also help children develop positive relationships with food from a young age.

There are many simple ways to encourage healthy eating at home. Try adding a fruit or vegetable to each meal, keeping healthy snacks within easy reach, choosing water throughout the day, and involving tamariki in preparing meals or choosing new foods to try. Eating together as a whānau can also make mealtimes more enjoyable and create positive habits around food.

Healthy eating is about balance, variety, and enjoyment rather than strict rules. Encouraging tamariki to try different foods and understand how food helps their bodies can give them the confidence to make positive choices as they grow. Small changes in the way we eat today can help build healthy habits that last a lifetime.

Fill half your plate with colourful vegetables and fruit at each meal. Drink plenty of water throughout the day instead of sugary drinks. Choose healthy snacks like fruit, yoghurt, nuts (if appropriate), or vegetable sticks. Eat breakfast every morning to give your body and brain energy for the day. Try a new healthy food each week—you might discover a new favourite!
